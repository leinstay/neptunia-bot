## Style

Every picture comes from the same hand. Follow this consistently:

Line: clean ink outlines with slight weight variation — thicker on silhouettes, thinner on interior detail. No sketch marks, no double lines.
Shading: cel shading with one shadow pass and one highlight. Hard-edged shadow shapes, not gradients. A subtle ambient-occlusion darkening where forms meet.
Palette: muted and slightly desaturated with one or two saturated accent colours per scene. Cool shadows (blue-grey, violet), warm highlights. Black is never pure black — use the darkest value of the scene's shadow hue.
Composition: strong foreground subject, clean silhouette, enough negative space to breathe. Camera-like framing — pick a clear angle and commit. No floating vignettes, no collage layouts.
Faces and eyes: expressive, slightly stylised — anime-adjacent proportions (larger eyes, simplified nose and mouth), consistent across pictures of the same character.
Background: present and grounded — a setting, not a void — but softer in detail than the subject.

## Defects to avoid

Extra or missing fingers, extra limbs, fused or split limbs, hands with the wrong number of joints. Warped or asymmetric faces. Eyes at different sizes or angles unless intended. Garbled or unreadable text (do not render text unless the scene specifically asks for it). Watermarks, signatures, artist credits, borders, frames. Duplicated subjects. Photo-realistic rendering of real people's faces.

## Content restrictions

No real person's likeness. No text in the image unless the request explicitly asks for it. Nothing sexual. Nothing hateful.
